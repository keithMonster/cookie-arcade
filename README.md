# Cookie's Arcade

A toddler-friendly mini-game collection for a (now 2-year-old) who started out smashing the keyboard. Single-file HTML per game, zero network, deployed on GitHub Pages.

## Live

https://keithmonster.github.io/cookie-arcade/

## Games (26)

| # | Name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **出门喽 Outing** | Multi-step pretend-play routine: a baby sits on the floor with four things scattered around — a shirt, a bottle, a cap and a little car — and **any order works**. Drag the shirt onto the baby and it slips on with a wriggle ("衣服穿好啦！"); **press and hold** the bottle and the milk really drains as the bottle tilts, glugging every beat until it's empty; drag the cap onto the head; tap the car and the baby climbs in. Nothing is ever wrong — a missed drag just slides home and quietly counts toward a helping hand, letting go of the bottle too early simply refills it with no scolding sound. Go quiet for 8s and one thing you *haven't* done yet lights up. All four done → the car drives off through the door with confetti, and a new baby arrives with a different shirt and cap. First multi-step routine on the台 where **every step is a different action** — the earlier multi-step games all repeat one action, this one is a whole errand. Also the first to use press-and-hold. |
| 2 | **帮帮忙 Little Helper** | Two-step-instruction listening: three things sit on a shelf and two animals wait below, then one whole sentence names both jobs at once — "先拿苹果，再给小兔子！" — and it never pauses in the middle. Tap the named thing and it flies down into a pair of waiting hands with a ding ("拿到苹果啦！"); now tap — or drag it onto — the named animal and it lands in their arms, hearts burst and they say "小兔子说：谢谢你！". Tap the wrong thing and it wobbles ("咦，这不是苹果呀！"); go for an animal before you're holding anything and it shakes its head ("先拿东西哦！") — information about what was asked for, never a scolding. Miss twice on either step and the right one pulses with light while the whole sentence plays again; go quiet for 8s and whatever you should be touching gives a flash. Both jobs done → confetti, and a new trio and pair come out with a different thing to fetch. First two-step-instruction game on the台 — 排排队 calls one animal at a time, this one says both halves before you may move. |
| 3 | **分一分 Sort by Color** | Sort-by-attribute: two big saturated bins sit on the table — say a red one and a blue one — and 4–6 identical toys scatter above them in those two colors. A voice opens each round by lighting up each bin in turn: "红色的，放这里！" … "蓝色的，放这里！". Drag a toy into the matching bin and it shrinks inside with a ding, the bin bounces and the voice names the color again — "红色的！". Drop it in the wrong bin and that bin shakes its head, the toy slides home and the voice tells you what color the toy actually is ("咦，这个是红色的呀！") — information, never a scolding. Miss twice with the same toy and the right bin pulses with light. Everything sorted → both bins jump, confetti, and a new color pair with a new toy comes out. First color-matching game on the台, and the first with two drop targets to choose between. |
| 4 | **跟着敲 Echo the Beat** | Call-and-response drumming: a bunny taps out a short pattern on its little drum — 2 to 4 beats, dots lighting up one by one — then says "该你啦！" and the big drum glows. Tap it (anywhere on the lower screen counts) and each hit fills a dot green with a deep thump; match the count and the bunny jumps, confetti flies and a new pattern comes. Only the *number* of beats matters — timing is never judged, and going quiet for 6s just makes the bunny play it again. First structured-rhythm game on the台, and the first built on turn-taking: 听我 / 该你. |
| 5 | **排排队 All Aboard** | Listen-and-sequence: a little train pulls in and three animals wait on the grass, then a voice calls them aboard one at a time — "小猫，先上车！" → "轮到小狗啦！" → "最后是大象！". Tap the right animal and it hops into its carriage with a whistle toot; tap one out of turn and it shakes its head ("还没轮到我呀！"), the right one glowing after 2 misses. All three aboard → the train chuffs off with smoke and confetti, and a new trio pulls in. First sequence-memory game on the台 — the point is the words 先 / 轮到 / 最后. |
| 6 | **装一装 Fill the Basket** | Drag-and-drop counting: 3–5 fruits scatter on the grass, drag each into the basket and a voice counts along — "一个！两个！三个！" — with a ding and a basket wiggle per drop. When the last one lands: "哇！三个苹果，全部装进来啦！" + confetti, then a new round with a different fruit. No right or wrong — a missed drop just slides back. Taps make fruits hop; if the player only taps (drag not there yet), the third tap flies the fruit in by itself. First drag-to-target interaction and first number-sense game on the台. |
| 7 | **睡觉觉 Sleep Tight** | Tuck-in pretend play: a sleepy animal sways on its little bed, tap anywhere and the blanket pulls up to its chin — the sky turns to night, stars come out, a soft "嘘，晚安，小猫，做个好梦" plays over a three-note lullaby while 💤 drifts up. Extra taps float quiet stars instead of waking it. Then a gentle sunrise brings the next of 8 animals. The wind-down closer of the care trilogy (bath → feed → sleep), deliberately low-stimulation. |
| 8 | **洗澡澡 Bath Time** | Scrub-to-clean pretend play: an animal shows up mucky and gray with mud blobs, scrub (tap or drag) to pop them off with splashes and squeaks, and when the last one goes the animal brightens up, bounces and hears "哇！小狗洗得干干净净！". 8 animals, shuffled queue. |
| 9 | **喂一喂 Feed Me** | Food-matching pretend play: a big animal waits hungry over three food cards, but only its favorite gets eaten — tap it and it flies into its mouth with a heart burst ("小猫最爱吃小鱼啦！"). Three right bites and it's full — burp, celebration, next animal. 8 animals, each with a signature food. |
| 10 | **送回家 Shape Home** | Shape matching: a little house has a shape-shaped hole, a voice asks "圆形在哪里呀？", tap the right of two shape cards and it flies in and clicks into place. Wrong taps wobble + replay; after 2 misses the right card glows. Always ends in success. 6 shapes. |

## Navigation

- Tap a tile, press `1`–`9` for an exact game, or any other key for a random one
- Inside a game: `Esc` or the top-left corner returns home; `F` toggles fullscreen

## On iPad

Open the live URL in Safari → **Share → Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen standalone app. Everything responds to touch.

## Audio

All spoken voice follows one contract:

- Generate mp3 files at build time with Volcengine TTS V3, voice 灿灿 (`zh_female_cancan_uranus_bigtts`, mono 24kHz). The game never calls TTS at runtime, so it still works offline.
- `tools/gen-audio.py` is the single source of truth for voice settings and spoken copy. Add a new game's manifest there, run `python3 tools/gen-audio.py --game <game-directory>`, then run the same command with `--check` before committing. Credentials come from `DOUBAO_TTS_APP_ID` / `DOUBAO_TTS_ACCESS_KEY` or the local voice-reply skill fallback; never commit them.
- Do not use `SpeechSynthesis` for spoken Chinese: it has been silent on the target iPad and would break the shared voice. Web Audio remains appropriate for non-speech effects.
- For sequential or autoplay-follow-up speech on iOS, reuse one `Audio` element and swap `src`; `games/words/` is the reference implementation.

## Structure

```
.
├── index.html              # arcade home (game picker)
├── games/
│   ├── _lib/               # Twirlywoos characters + stage + shared word data
│   ├── helper/             # two-step instruction: fetch X then give it to Y (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── sort/               # drag-by-color into two bins (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── echo/               # call-and-response drumming (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── train/              # all-aboard sequence game (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── basket/             # drag-fruits-into-basket counting (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── sleep/              # tuck-in bedtime (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── bath/               # scrub-to-clean bath time (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── feed/               # pretend feeding (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── shapes/             # shape matching (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── find/               # listen-and-point (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── words/              # word cards (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── cars/  animals/     # tap-to-spawn games (audio/ = pre-rendered mp3)
│   ├── tw-summon/ tw-bubbles/ tw-chorus/ tw-mood/ tw-more/
│   ├── peekaboo/ storybook/ scribble/
│   └── keyboard/ bubbles/ drums/ cascade/   # the original four, fully self-contained
└── tools/                  # offline audio generation (gen-audio.py, Volcengine TTS + ffmpeg)
```

v1 games are self-contained single files; the Twirlywoos five share `_lib/`, and words/find share one word list (`_lib/words-data.js`). `.nojekyll` keeps GitHub Pages from eating the `_lib` directory.
